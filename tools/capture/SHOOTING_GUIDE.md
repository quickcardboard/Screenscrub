# ScreenScrub capture: shooting guide

## Setup (once per screen)
1. Copy this folder to a laptop and open `index.html` in Chrome or Edge.
   - **TV:** connect the laptop by HDMI and use the TV as the display, or cast the browser tab.
   - **Laptop / monitor / tablet:** open `index.html` on that device.
2. TV only: set picture size to **"Just Scan"**, **"Screen Fit"** or **"1:1"**, so the four corner markers are visible.
   Leave the picture mode as you normally use it.
3. Turn off screen dimming or sleep, and plug the device in.
4. Press **Start**. Slides change every 3 seconds. Tap or press Space to pause.

## Shooting (about 2 minutes per pass)
Use the normal Camera app: no portrait mode, no filters. **Hold the shutter for a burst** and move slowly.

| Pass | Lighting | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Room lights on | Whole screen in frame. Sweep slowly left to right (up to ~30° off-centre each way), a little up and down. |
| 2 | Same | **Close-ups:** walk in until the screen overfills the frame, drift around. Try 2× zoom for part of it. |
| 3 | Lamp or window **behind you**, so a reflection shows | Mix of whole screen and close-ups. |
| 4 | Dark room | Mix of whole screen and close-ups. |

A few times per pass, also:
- tilt the phone so the screen looks rotated 5–15°;
- step back so the screen is small in the frame;
- shoot from a sharp angle (like across a room or a bar).

## Screens (more variety beats more photos)
TV first, then any of: a second TV, laptop, monitor, tablet, phone. 2–3 screens is a good start; 4–5 is better.

## Also useful
- **20–30 real-life shots without the slideshow** (TV at a friend's, a public display). These have no ground truth;
  they are the test set for judging the results.
- **Your own screenshots** of things you actually share: put them in a folder and send them over.
  They get added as slides.

## Afterwards
Upload the photos, one folder per screen and pass (e.g. `tv_pass1/`), to the `screenscrub` Google Drive folder.
Blurry frames and frames caught between slides are discarded automatically, so don't sort them yourself.
